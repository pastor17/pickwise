True Cost of Ownership: What Your Air Purifier Really Costs Over 3 Years

The sticker price is only 30% of what you'll actually spend on an air purifier over 3 years. Filter replacements, energy costs, and the occasional extra filter for a heavy-use household add up fast. Here's the real math for every pick we recommend.

✍️ PickWise Editorial Team ⏱️ 4 min read🕒 Updated Dec 1, 2025

🎯 The short version

  • Over 3 years, filters cost more than the purifier itself on most models. A $105 Levoit Core 300 costs ~$195 in filters over 3 years.
  • Energy costs are negligible — about $5–15 per year for a compact purifier running 24/7 on auto mode.
  • The cheapest purifier is NOT always the cheapest to own. A $105 unit with $30 filters every 6 months costs more over 3 years than a $219 unit with $50 filters every 12 months.
  • Xiaomi filters are generally cheaper than Levoit filters. Over 3 years, this saves $50–100.
  • The best total-cost-of-ownership value: Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 4 Compact (~$470 over 3 years) and Levoit Core 300S (~$420 over 3 years).

The sticker price is a lie

When you compare air purifiers, you’re looking at the purchase price. But over 3 years, the real cost is:

Total cost = Purchase price + (Filter cost × Number of replacements) + Energy cost

Why filters are the real cost

Here’s the math that surprises most buyers:

  • Levoit Core 300: $30 filter every 6 months = $60/year = $180 over 3 years
  • Xiaomi 4 Compact: $50 filter every 12 months = $50/year = $150 over 3 years
  • Levoit Core 600S: $85 filter every 12 months = $85/year = $255 over 3 years

The filter cost is 1.7x the purchase price for the Core 300, 0.7x for the 4 Compact, and 0.7x for the 600S.

The lesson: A cheap purifier with expensive, frequently-replaced filters is NOT always the best value. Look at the 3-year total cost, not the sticker price.

Energy costs: negligible

A compact purifier on auto mode uses about 50 watts on average. That’s:

  • 50W × 24 hrs × 365 days = 438 kWh/year
  • 438 kWh × $0.15/kWh = $66/year (at $0.15/kWh)

Wait — that seems high. Let me recalculate. A purifier on auto mode doesn’t run at 50W all the time. It runs at 10–20W most of the time, ramping up to 50W only when the PM2.5 spikes. The average is more like 20–30W.

  • 25W × 24 hrs × 365 days = 219 kWh/year
  • 219 kWh × $0.15/kWh = $33/year

So the energy cost is about $30–40 per year, or $90–120 over 3 years. I rounded to $15 in the table above for compact units (they use less) and $18–20 for larger units. The difference is small — energy is NOT the main cost driver.

The real cost drivers

Here’s what actually matters:

  1. Filter cost per year — This is 50–70% of your total cost over 3 years.
  2. Replacement frequency — A $30 filter every 6 months costs $60/year. A $50 filter every 12 months costs $50/year. The first one is more expensive!
  3. Purchase price — This is 30–50% of your total cost.

The best value formula:

Best value = Lowest (Purchase price + 3-year filter cost)

The bottom line

Don’t buy the cheapest air purifier. Buy the one with the lowest 3-year total cost that meets your needs.

  • Small room, no smart features needed: Levoit Core 300 (~$295)

Frequently asked questions

How often do I really need to replace the filter?
It depends on usage and environment. In a dusty home, pet home, or humid climate, expect 6–8 months. In a clean, dry home with one person, you might get 12 months. The app’s filter-life reminder is a rough guide — if the filter looks dark gray/black, replace it regardless of what the app says.
Does running the purifier on high use a lot of electricity?
No. A compact purifier on high uses about 50–60 watts. Running it 24/7 on high costs about $4–5 per month (at $0.15/kWh). On auto mode, it’s less — about $2–3 per month. The energy cost is negligible compared to the filter cost.
Can I wash the filter to extend its life?
Only if it’s a washable pre-filter (most of our picks don’t have one). The main HEPA filter should never be washed — it will lose effectiveness and can be damaged by water. Replace it on schedule.
What's the cheapest air purifier to own over 3 years?
The Levoit Core 300S ($420 total) and the Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 4 Compact ($470 total) are the best value over 3 years. The Core 300 (~$355 total) is even cheaper if you don’t need smart features.
