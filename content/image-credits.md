We use imagery that is free of watermarks and properly licensed. Here's where it comes from.

## Product images

- **Amazon Product Advertising API** — official product images, provided for use by Amazon Associates. This is our primary source for product shots. See `scripts/amazon_images.py` in the site repository.
- **Manufacturer press & spec pages** — official product photography provided by brands for editorial use.

## Lifestyle imagery

- **Unsplash and similar royalty-free libraries** — licensed for commercial use without attribution requirements.

## A note on watermarks

We never publish watermarked images, and we do **not** remove watermarks from images. Removing a watermark strips attribution and circumvents copyright protection, which is both legally risky and against our editorial policy. Instead, we source images that are already watermark-free and properly licensed from the outset.

## Questions?

If you believe an image on this site is misattributed or should be removed, [contact us](/contact/) and we'll address it promptly.
