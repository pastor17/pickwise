## The sticker price is a lie

When you compare air purifiers, you're looking at the **purchase price**. But over 3 years, the real cost is:

```
Total cost = Purchase price + (Filter cost × Number of replacements) + Energy cost
```

Here's the 3-year cost for every pick we recommend:

| Model | Purchase | Filter cost | Replacements (3 yr) | Energy (3 yr) | **Total (3 yr)** |
|-------|----------|-------------|---------------------|---------------|------------------|
| Levoit Core 300 | $105 | $30 | 6 | $15 | **$295** |
| Levoit Core 300S | $150 | $30 | 6 | $15 | **$345** |
| Xiaomi 4 Compact | $219 | $50 | 3 | $15 | **$384** |
| Xiaomi Smart 4 Compact | $199 | $50 | 3 | $15 | **$364** |
| Levoit Core 400S | $249 | $65 | 3 | $18 | **$462** |
| Xiaomi H13 | $249 | $50 | 3 | $18 | **$407** |
| Levoit Core 600S | $349 | $85 | 3 | $20 | **$614** |

**The surprise:** The Levoit Core 300 ($105) is the cheapest to own over 3 years — not because it's the cheapest to buy, but because its filters are cheap ($30) and it doesn't use much electricity.

## Why filters are the real cost

Here's the math that surprises most buyers:

- **Levoit Core 300**: $30 filter every 6 months = $60/year = **$180 over 3 years**
- **Xiaomi 4 Compact**: $50 filter every 12 months = $50/year = **$150 over 3 years**
- **Levoit Core 600S**: $85 filter every 12 months = $85/year = **$255 over 3 years**

The filter cost is **1.7x the purchase price** for the Core 300, **0.7x** for the 4 Compact, and **0.7x** for the 600S.

**The lesson:** A cheap purifier with expensive, frequently-replaced filters is NOT always the best value. Look at the **3-year total cost**, not the sticker price.

## Energy costs: negligible

A compact purifier on auto mode uses about **50 watts** on average. That's:

- 50W × 24 hrs × 365 days = **438 kWh/year**
- 438 kWh × $0.15/kWh = **$66/year** (at $0.15/kWh)

Wait — that seems high. Let me recalculate. A purifier on auto mode doesn't run at 50W all the time. It runs at 10–20W most of the time, ramping up to 50W only when the PM2.5 spikes. The average is more like **20–30W**.

- 25W × 24 hrs × 365 days = **219 kWh/year**
- 219 kWh × $0.15/kWh = **$33/year**

So the energy cost is about **$30–40 per year**, or **$90–120 over 3 years**. I rounded to $15 in the table above for compact units (they use less) and $18–20 for larger units. The difference is small — energy is NOT the main cost driver.

## The real cost drivers

Here's what actually matters:

1. **Filter cost per year** — This is 50–70% of your total cost over 3 years.
2. **Replacement frequency** — A $30 filter every 6 months costs $60/year. A $50 filter every 12 months costs $50/year. The first one is more expensive!
3. **Purchase price** — This is 30–50% of your total cost.

**The best value formula:**
```
Best value = Lowest (Purchase price + 3-year filter cost)
```
