PickWise exists for one reason: **buying the wrong thing is expensive and annoying.** We help you avoid it.

We're an independent, reader-supported review site. We research, compare, and — where possible — test the products people actually buy for their homes, so you can decide with confidence instead of guessing.

**Our focus: the best-value Chinese brands.** Chinese manufacturers have built some of the best air purifiers in the world — same H13 True HEPA filtration, same CADR performance, often at a lower price. But most of the Chinese market's top products (352, IAM, 华为智选, 美的) are China-only. We focus on the brands that **actually ship to overseas buyers** — Xiaomi, Levoit — and give you the honest, no-nonsense reviews you need to buy with confidence.

## What we believe

- **Specs beat marketing.** "Hospital-grade," "whisper-quiet," and "covers 1,500 sq ft" are claims. CADR, decibels, and filter costs are facts. We deal in facts first.
- **Every product has flaws.** A review that only praises isn't a review — it's an ad. We tell you what we *don't* like about every product we cover.
- **Price isn't the whole story.** A $90 purifier with $60/year filters can cost more than a $200 one with $40/year filters. We count total cost of ownership.
- **Independence is non-negotiable.** We don't accept payment for placement. Ever.

## How we're funded

PickWise is supported by affiliate commissions. When you buy through our links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That's how we keep the lights on — but it never changes what we write. [Read our full disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
