## At a glance

The Levoit Core 300S is the smart sibling of the Core 300 — same excellent filtration, same quiet operation, but with Wi-Fi, a companion app, and voice control. At a street price around **$150**, it's the best smart purifier you can buy for a bedroom or small room without stepping into $250+ territory.

**The "S" stands for "Smart"** — and it's the reason to pay the premium over the regular Core 300.

## A Chinese brand, globally trusted

Levoit (深圳市乐沃科技有限公司) is a Chinese home-appliance company that has built a global reputation on value and reliability. The Core 300S is their flagship compact smart purifier, sold in over 50 countries. A few things about the brand matter for buyers:

- **Filter availability** — Levoit ships replacement filters to most countries via Amazon. You won't be stuck waiting for a Chinese import to replace your filter.
- **Price point** — Levoit's scale and supply-chain efficiency let them price aggressively. The Core 300S costs less than the Coway AP-1512HH and delivers smart features the Coway doesn't have.
- **Build quality** — consistent across markets. The US-spec unit is the same quality as the China-spec unit.

## Design and build quality

The Core 300S is a squat cylinder, about 14.5 inches tall, that disappears against a wall. The white matte finish resists fingerprints, and the top-mounted touch panel has three buttons: Power, Fan, and a mode toggle. You'll likely use the app more than the physical buttons.

The 360° top-intake and output design means you can place it against a wall or in a corner without choking the airflow. It's light enough to carry from the bedroom to a home office without a second thought.

## Performance in real use

With a smoke CADR of roughly **141 CFM**, the Core 300S is best matched to rooms around **200–220 sq ft**. In a standard bedroom (10×12 ft), it cycles the air comfortably on medium or low. In a large living room it's out of its depth — you'd want a higher-CADR model.

The H13 True HEPA filter captures **99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns** — pollen, pet dander, dust mite debris, mold spores, and smoke particles. The thin carbon layer helps with light odors but won't meaningfully tackle VOCs or heavy cooking smells.

## Noise

On the lowest "Sleep" setting, the Core 300S runs at about **24 dB** — quieter than a whisper. It's genuinely bedroom-friendly; you can run it all night without disturbing a light sleeper. On high it reaches ~50 dB, which is noticeable but not harsh.

## Filters and running costs

The Core 300S uses a single combined filter that Levoit recommends replacing every **6–8 months**, at around **$30 a pop**. Run it 24/7 and you're looking at roughly **$60–80 per year** in filters. There's no washable pre-filter, so dust loads the HEPA faster in dusty homes.

The filter is easy to find — it's stocked on Amazon in most countries, so you won't be waiting weeks for a replacement.

## Smart features

This is where the "S" earns its keep:

- **Wi-Fi connectivity** — connect to your home network via the Levoit app
- **Companion app** — air-quality readouts, scheduling, remote control, and filter-life tracking
- **Alexa and Google Assistant** — "Hey Google, turn on the bedroom purifier" works out of the box
- **Auto mode** — the unit adjusts fan speed based on its built-in air-quality sensor

The app is clean and responsive. You can set it to ramp up at bedtime, run harder during pollen season, or just leave it on auto and forget about it.

## Who should buy it

- Renters and students who want a **smart, affordable purifier** for a bedroom or dorm
- Smart-home enthusiasts who want voice control without paying $250+
- People with mild allergies who want the convenience of auto mode
- Anyone who wants a set-it-and-forget-it unit that runs all night

## Who should skip it

- Large-room or open-plan living — you'll want higher CADR
- Heavy odor or VOC concerns — the carbon layer is too thin
- People who prefer a washable pre-filter to reduce filter-replacement frequency
- Non-smart-home users — the regular Core 300 is the same unit for ~$45 less

## The bottom line

The Core 300S is the **best smart purifier under $160**. It's quiet, effective, and genuinely convenient. The Levoit brand (a Chinese company with a global footprint) means you can find replacement filters easily, and the price means you can afford a second unit for another room.

If you want a bedroom purifier that you can set, forget, and never think about again, this is the one.
