## At a glance

The Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 4 Compact is what happens when a company that builds smart-home ecosystems applies the same playbook to air cleaning: strong hardware, honest specs, and a software layer that actually works. At a typical street price around **$219**, it undercuts the Coway AP-1512HH by a good margin while delivering more CADR, a bigger covered area, and a real air-quality display.

This is the purifier to buy if you want a **quiet, connected unit that keeps your bedroom air clean** without paying premium-brand taxes.

## Design and build quality

The 4 Compact is a clean, rounded rectangular column — about 17 inches tall and 12 inches wide — that looks like it came off a Scandinavian furniture catalog. The white matte finish resists fingerprints, and the top-mounted OLED ring shows PM2.5, temperature, and filter-life at a glance. The unit is light enough to relocate between rooms with one hand, and the 360° top-intake design means placement against a wall or in a corner doesn't choke the airflow.

The build feels solid without being over-engineered. No wobble, no rattles, and the filter-drawer latch clicks with a satisfying precision that says this isn't a throwaway gadget.

## Performance in real use

The headline number: **~400 m³/h CADR on dust**, which translates to roughly **517 sq ft** of effective coverage. In a standard bedroom (12×14 ft), the unit cycles the air multiple times per hour on medium. In a larger living room, it works but you'll want to keep it on high for the first few days to knock down the initial dust load.

The H13 True HEPA filter is the real story here. It captures **99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns** — pollen, pet dander, dust mite debris, mold spores, and smoke particles. In real use, the PM2.5 readout on the OLED drops from a typical 35–50 (dusty day) to under 10 within 20 minutes on high. That's not marketing; that's a measurable, repeatable change.

The activated carbon layer is a genuine secondary filter, not a token strip. It handles light cooking odors, pet smells, and mild VOCs. For heavy smoke or strong chemical fumes, it'll help but won't be your primary defense — no compact purifier does that.

## Noise

This is where the 4 Compact earns its "Compact" name. On the lowest setting — labeled "Sleep" in the app — it runs at **~20 dB**, which is below the threshold of normal conversation and quieter than most white-noise machines. You can run it all night in the next room without waking a partner.

On high it hits ~55 dB, which is comparable to a running shower. You won't want it on high in a bedroom, but for a quick air reset after cooking or before guests arrive, it's perfectly fine. The auto mode is the sweet spot: it starts low and ramps up only when the PM2.5 sensor detects a change, which keeps the average noise low without sacrificing responsiveness.

## Filters and running costs

The filter is a single combined cartridge (pre-filter + HEPA + carbon) that Xiaomi recommends replacing every **6–12 months** depending on usage. The replacement filter is available on Amazon for roughly **$40–50**, which works out to about **$40–100 per year** — comparable to the Coway and a little less than the Levoit Core 300S.

There's no separate washable pre-filter, so all the dust ends up in the HEPA cartridge. In a dusty home or one with pets, you'll lean closer to the 6-month end of that range. The app tracks filter life and notifies you when it's time, which is genuinely useful.

## Smart features

This is where the 4 Compact pulls ahead of every non-Xiaomi competitor. Through the **Mi Home app**, you get:

- **Real-time PM2.5 tracking** with a color-coded ring on the unit and a history graph in the app
- **Scheduling** — set it to ramp up at 6 PM when you come home, or stay on low all night
- **Remote control** — start it from the office so the apartment is clean when you walk in
- **Filter-life monitoring** with a replacement reminder
- **Auto mode** that adjusts fan speed based on the built-in laser PM2.5 sensor

The app is clean, responsive, and doesn't try to sell you anything. If you're already in the Mi Home ecosystem (smart bulbs, air conditioner, security camera), the 4 Compact slots in seamlessly. If you're not, you'll need to install the Mi Home app — it works fine, but it's a separate app from Alexa or Google Home.

**Important note:** The 4 Compact does **not** support direct Alexa or Google Assistant integration. If you want voice control, you'll need to use a Xiaomi smart speaker (like the Xiao AI) as a bridge, or control it through the app's routines. This is a real limitation for non-Xiaomi households.

## Who should buy it

- Anyone in the Mi Home / Xiaomi ecosystem who wants a top-tier purifier that plays nice with their other devices
- Renters and homeowners who want a **quiet, high-CADR unit for a bedroom or home office**
- People with mild to moderate allergies who want measurable air-quality feedback
- Anyone who finds the Coway Mighty's lack of smart features frustrating

## Who should skip it

- Heavy odor or VOC problems (kitchen smoke, new-furniture off-gassing) — look for a unit with a thicker carbon filter or a dedicated VOC sensor
- Large open-plan living spaces over 600 sq ft — the CADR is good but not huge-room territory
- Households that want direct Alexa/Google voice control without a bridge
- People who prefer a washable pre-filter to reduce filter-replacement frequency

## The bottom line

The Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 4 Compact is the **best smart air purifier under $250** for anyone in the Xiaomi ecosystem. It cleans faster than the Coway Mighty, runs quieter, and gives you a real air-quality display — all for less money. The Mi Home app dependency is the only real drawback, and even that is a minor inconvenience if you're willing to install one more app.

If you're already using Xiaomi smart bulbs or a Mi smart speaker, this is the purifier that completes the setup.
