## At a glance

The Levoit Core 600S is the biggest, most powerful purifier in Levoit's Core series. At a street price around **$349**, it's the best air purifier for open-plan spaces under $400. If your living room, dining area, and kitchen are all one big open space, this is the unit that'll actually keep up with the air volume.

**The "600S" means it's the 600-series smart model** — the top of the Core lineup. If the Core 400S felt a bit underpowered for your space, this is the step up.

## A Chinese brand, globally trusted

Levoit (深圳市乐沃科技有限公司) is a Chinese home-appliance company with a 50+ country presence. The Core 600S is their flagship large-room purifier, sold on Amazon in the US, EU, and most major markets. It's rated 4.6 stars by 6,000+ Amazon reviewers.

## Design and build quality

The Core 600S is a tall, heavy cylinder — about 24 inches high and 10 inches in diameter, weighing around 30 lbs. It's the tallest and heaviest Levoit in the Core series, so you'll want to find a permanent spot (a corner of the living room, against a wall) rather than trying to move it between rooms.

The white matte finish matches the rest of the Core series, and the top-mounted color ring shows air quality at a glance. The build is solid — no wobble, no rattles, and the filter drawer latches cleanly.

## Performance in real use

The headline number: **375 CFM PM1 CADR** (AHAM Verifide certified). That's among the highest CADR ratings in the $300–$400 price range. For comparison:

| Model | CADR (dust) | Coverage |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Levoit Core 600S | ~273 CFM | ~635 sq ft (AHAM) |
| Levoit Core 400S | 240 CFM | ~635 sq ft (AHAM) |
| Coway AP-1512HH | 233 CFM | ~361 sq ft (AHAM) |
| Blueair Blue Pure 511 | 104 CFM | ~272 sq ft (AHAM) |

In real use, the Core 600S cleans a large open-plan living + dining area (40×30 ft, ~1,200 sq ft) to under 15 PM2.5 within 30 minutes on high. In a standard living room (20×20 ft, ~400 sq ft), it's overkill — the Core 400S is the better size.

The H13 True HEPA filter captures **99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns**. The **480g of activated carbon** is 2x what you get in the Core 400S, making it significantly better for odors, cooking smells, and VOCs. In a new home with off-gassing furniture, the extra carbon makes a real difference.

## Noise

On the lowest "Sleep" setting, the Core 600S runs at about **24 dB** — quieter than a whisper. For a unit this size, that's impressive.

On the highest setting it reaches ~60 dB, which is comparable to a vacuum cleaner. You won't want it on high in a bedroom, but for a quick air reset in a living room, it's fine. The auto mode is the sweet spot: it starts low and ramps up only when the PM2.5 sensor detects a change.

## Smart features

- **AirSight Plus** — real-time PM2.5 monitoring with a color-coded ring
- **Levoit app** — air-quality history, scheduling, remote control, filter-life tracking
- **Alexa and Google Assistant** — voice control out of the box
- **Sleep mode** — dims the display, locks out high-speed settings
- **Auto mode** — adjusts fan speed based on the built-in PM2.5 sensor
- **Remote control** — included in the box (handy if you don't want to use the app)

## Filters and running costs

The Core 600S uses a single combined filter (pre-filter + HEPA + carbon) that Levoit recommends replacing every **6–12 months**. The replacement filter costs about **$80–90** on Amazon.

That works out to **$80–180 per year** in filter costs. It's the highest in the Core series, but proportionally reasonable for a unit this size. There's no washable pre-filter, so dust loads the HEPA faster in dusty homes or pet homes.

## Who should buy it

- Anyone with an **open-plan living space** (800+ sq ft) who wants a single purifier to handle the whole area
- People in new homes with heavy off-gassing — the 480g carbon layer helps
- Large pet homes where dander is a constant issue
- Smart-home enthusiasts who want Alexa/Google control for a large room

## Who should skip it

- Small bedrooms or offices under 300 sq ft — the Core 300 or 300S is cheaper and does the job
- People on a tight budget — the Core 400S is $100 less and covers most living rooms
- Those who want a Mi Home ecosystem — the Xiaomi H13 has similar CADR for $249

## The bottom line

The Levoit Core 600S is the **best air purifier for open-plan spaces under $400**. It has the highest CADR in Levoit's Core series, 2x the carbon of the 400S, and the same smart features. If your living + dining + kitchen are all one big room, this is the unit that'll actually keep up.
