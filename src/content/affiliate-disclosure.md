**PickWise is reader-supported.** When you buy products through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission at **no additional cost to you**.

## What this means for you

- **You never pay more.** Our links don't change the price you pay — ever.
- **It never changes our opinion.** We don't accept payment for placement, and commission rates don't influence what we recommend. We'd rather earn your trust than a slightly larger commission.

## Our affiliate relationships

PickWise is a participant in the **Amazon Services LLC Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. We may also participate in other affiliate programs from time to time.

## How to spot our links

Affiliate links in our content are marked with `rel="sponsored"` where applicable, and our product buttons link out to retailers. The disclosure note appears on every article that contains such links.
