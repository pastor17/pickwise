## The short version

**Xiaomi** wins on performance, smart features, and CADR-per-dollar. **Levoit** wins on price, simplicity, and voice-control compatibility (Alexa/Google). For most buyers, the **Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 4 Compact** ($219) is the best overall value. For budget-focused buyers who want a no-frills purifier, the **Levoit Core 300** ($105) is unbeatable.

## The head-to-head

### 1. CADR: Xiaomi wins

| Model | CADR (dust) | Coverage |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Xiaomi 4 Compact | ~400 m³/h | ~517 sq ft |
| Xiaomi Smart 4 Compact | ~360 m³/h | ~517 sq ft |
| Xiaomi H13 | ~420 m³/h | ~538 sq ft |
| Levoit Core 300 | ~141 CFM (~220 m³/h) | ~219 sq ft |
| Levoit Core 300S | ~141 CFM (~220 m³/h) | ~219 sq ft |

The Xiaomi models have **2x the CADR** of the Levoit models. This means they clean a room faster and can handle larger spaces. If you have a room over 300 sq ft, the Xiaomi models are the better choice.

### 2. Noise: Levoit wins (slightly)

| Model | Low (dB) | High (dB) |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Xiaomi 4 Compact | 20 | 55 |
| Xiaomi Smart 4 Compact | 20 | 50 |
| Xiaomi H13 | 22 | 55 |
| Levoit Core 300 | 24 | 48 |
| Levoit Core 300S | 24 | 50 |

The Xiaomi Smart 4 Compact ties the Levoit models at 20 dB (the quietest). The standard Xiaomi 4 Compact is 20 dB on low, which is also excellent. The Levoit models are 24 dB, which is still very quiet but not quite as silent.

For most buyers, the difference between 20 and 24 dB is negligible. Both are quiet enough to run all night.

## The verdict by use case

### Best for a bedroom (200–300 sq ft)
**Winner: Levoit Core 300 ($105)**

You don't need 400 m³/h of CADR for a 200 sq ft bedroom. The Core 300's 141 CFM is plenty, and it costs half as much as the Xiaomi models. If you want smart features, step up to the Core 300S ($150).

### Best for a living room (400–600 sq ft)
**Winner: Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 4 Compact ($219)**

A 500 sq ft living room needs at least 330 m³/h of CADR. The Levoit models can't handle this space. The 4 Compact's 400 m³/h is the right size, and the Mi Home app is a bonus.

### Best for open-plan spaces (600+ sq ft)
**Winner: Xiaomi Air Purifier H13 ($249)**

The H13's 420 m³/h is the highest CADR in our comparison. For an open-plan living + dining area, this is the right size.

### Best for smart-home integration
**Winner: Xiaomi (all models)**

If you're in the Mi Home ecosystem, the Xiaomi models are the clear winner. The Mi Home app is more complete than Levoit's, and the Xiaomi purifiers integrate with 200+ other devices.

### Best for Alexa/Google users
**Winner: Levoit Core 300S ($150)**

If you're in the Google Home or Alexa ecosystem (and not in Mi Home), the Core 300S is the only model in our comparison with direct voice-control integration.

### Best budget pick
**Winner: Levoit Core 300 ($105)**

At $105, it's the cheapest True HEPA purifier you can buy. No smart features, no app, no Wi-Fi — just a purifier that cleans the air. For a small room, it's the best value.

## The bottom line

**Xiaomi** is the better brand if you want **performance, smart features, and a complete ecosystem**. The 4 Compact ($219) is the best overall value in the compact/medium size range.

**Levoit** is the better brand if you want **simplicity, affordability, and direct voice control**. The Core 300 ($105) is the best budget pick, and the Core 300S ($150) is the best smart pick for Alexa/Google users.

**For most buyers:** The **Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 4 Compact** is the best choice. It has the best CADR, the best smart integration, and a price that makes sense. The only reason to choose Levoit is if you're on a tight budget or you're firmly in the Alexa/Google ecosystem.
